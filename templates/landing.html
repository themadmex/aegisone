Why 21 questions instead of 20?

Twenty covers the core controls across accounts, devices, backups, email, and incident response — and Question 21 (yes, a nod to the song) asks the ultimate real-world test: if a work laptop disappeared tomorrow, would your backups and remote sign-out still have your back?

Do we need an IT specialist to start?

No. A workspace owner can start in plain English, mark technical items as “Unsure,” and optionally invite an internal or external IT contributor to help verify and complete actions.

Does AegisOne install device agents or scan internal systems?

Never. AegisOne combines your guided self-assessment with optional, owner-authorized public DNS checks (SPF and DMARC) after you publish a verification TXT record.

Who can view our workspace score and reports?

Only active, MFA-verified members of your workspace. There are no public report links, and revoking a member removes their access on the very next request.
